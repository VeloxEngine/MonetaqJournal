Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026 • Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Monetaq Tech Journal ("Monetaq," "we," "us," or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, processing, storage, and safeguarding of information obtained when you access or interact with our website, publications, technical articles, and associated digital services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with any aspect of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the Services immediately.

2. Data Controller Information

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and applicable data protection legislation in other jurisdictions, the data controller responsible for your personal data is:

Monetaq Tech Publications
Legal & Privacy Compliance Operations
Email: privacy@monetaq.example.com

3. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of strict data minimization. We only collect information necessary to deliver, protect, and optimize our technical publication.

3.1. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you navigate through the website, our edge hosting servers and reverse proxies (including Cloudflare and Vercel) automatically record standard network request metadata in server access logs:

  • Network Identifiers: Internet Protocol (IP) address (which may be truncated or pseudonymized).
  • Device & User Agent Information: Browser type, operating system version, browser language preference, screen resolution, and hardware architecture.
  • Navigation Telemetry: Uniform Resource Locators (URLs) requested, referring URLs, HTTP status codes, timestamp of access, and data volume transferred.

3.2. Cookies and Local Client Storage

We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising tracking cookies. We utilize minimal first-party cookies and browser localStorage strictly for essential functional operations, such as caching user theme preferences (dark mode defaults) and storing UI session state.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

Under European data protection laws, we process personal information relying upon the following lawful bases:

  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Operating, debugging, and securing our digital infrastructure; mitigating denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks; preventing fraud; and analyzing aggregated traffic patterns.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): Retaining records where mandated by applicable statutory accounting, tax, or legal discovery laws.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where you explicitly submit your email address for technical newsletters or developer updates. You possess the right to revoke consent at any time.

5. Third-Party Service Providers and Processors

We engage reputable cloud infrastructure vendors to maintain uptime and performance. These entities process telemetry data under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Cloudflare Inc.: Anycast Content Delivery Network (CDN), DDoS mitigation, and edge proxying.
  • Vercel Inc.: Application hosting, edge compute runtimes, and build pipelines.
  • Supabase Inc.: Managed PostgreSQL database infrastructure and identity authentication layers.

6. International Data Transfers

Because our infrastructure utilizes globally distributed edge points of presence, data may be processed on servers situated outside your country of residence (including the United States). Where international transfers occur, we implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission to ensure adequate protection.

7. Data Retention

Server access logs containing IP addresses are retained for operational diagnosis and security auditing for a maximum period of thirty (30) days, after which they are permanently overwritten or anonymized, unless a security incident requires ongoing investigation.

8. Your Legal Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you possess specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation and copies of personal data maintained about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of personal data when no legal justification remains for retention.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Request suspension of processing based on legitimate interest grounds.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain a structured, commonly used, machine-readable copy of your provided data.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

9. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

To exercise your statutory data protection rights or submit questions regarding this policy, contact our Data Privacy Officer via email at privacy@monetaq.example.com.
